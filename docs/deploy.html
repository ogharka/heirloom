Deploy the Heirloom contract

This puts the Heirloom contract on Arc mainnet from your browser wallet. You do it once.

  1. Use a fresh wallet that holds only a few USDC on Arc, not your main wallet.
  2. Click deploy. Your wallet will show a contract deployment costing well under 1 USDC in gas, with nothing sent to any other address.
  3. Approve it. The page shows your contract address when Arc confirms it.

Heirloom never asks for your seed phrase or private key, and neither does anyone legitimate. If anything does, close the page.
