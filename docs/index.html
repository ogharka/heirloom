Heirloom

If you go quiet, your USDC goes home.

Heirloom is an inheritance vault on Arc. Lock USDC for the people you choose and check in now and then. If you stop checking in, each heir can claim their share. No lawyer, no custodian, and no seed phrase to pass on.

  1. Lock USDCName your heirs and how much each one gets.
  2. Check inOne tap resets the timer. You can withdraw any time.
  3. Heirs claimMiss the window and each heir claims their share.
